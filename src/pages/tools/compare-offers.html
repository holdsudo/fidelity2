What to compare (besides the rate)

  • Net proceeds — what actually lands in your account after fees.
  • Total payback — the full amount you’ll repay.
  • Payment size and frequency — can your daily or weekly cash flow carry it comfortably?
  • Early payoff terms — some funders discount if you pay early; others don’t.
