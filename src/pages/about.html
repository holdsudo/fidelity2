Built for the businesses banks left behind.

In these challenging times, traditional banks have made it harder than ever for small and medium-sized businesses to access capital. Fidelity Funding was built to change that.

Through our network of trusted national funding partners, we provide the flexibility and speed that modern businesses need — without the red tape or endless waiting. Whether you’re just starting out or scaling fast, Fidelity Funding has the expertise and connections to help you secure the financing your business deserves.

How we work

  • We’re a broker, not a lender. We match you with funding partners and help you compare real offers — so you’re not stuck with one option.
  • Soft pull first. Your initial review doesn’t affect your credit score.
  • Plain-English clarity. Total payback, payment schedule and term — upfront, for every offer.
  • Real people. A dedicated funding specialist guides you from application to funding.

Our editorial standards

The guides on this site are written and reviewed by the Fidelity Funding team to help owners make informed decisions. We don’t publish invented statistics, and we don’t promise approvals, rates or timelines — those are always set by funding partners after underwriting. Our content is general information, not financial, legal or tax advice.
