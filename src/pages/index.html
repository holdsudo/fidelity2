FAQ

Questions, answered.

Still curious?

How long does it take to get approved and funded?

Most applicants receive an initial decision within hours of submitting their information. Once approved, funds can often be deposited in as little as 24 hours, depending on the loan type and documentation required.

Will applying affect my credit score?

No — your initial application and funding analysis use a soft credit pull, meaning it does not impact your credit score. A hard inquiry is only performed if you decide to move forward with a specific lender.

What can I use the funds for?

Almost anything related to your business! From covering payroll and inventory to expanding locations or upgrading equipment — these are just examples. You’re free to use your funding however it best supports your business.

What types of businesses does Fidelity Funding work with?

We work with a wide range of small and mid-sized businesses across every industry — including retail, construction, transportation, healthcare, restaurants, and more. Whether you’re a startup or an established company, we’ll help you find the right financing solution.
